**veto.fun** is the memecoin launchpad on **Arc**, Circle's USDC-native chain. One transaction launches your token **straight into a live Uniswap v3 pool** with the entire supply locked as liquidity forever. No bonding curve, no graduation, no waiting: it's a real DEX token from block one, priced in USDC and visible to every major trading platform that watches Uniswap. **You hold the keys. The market decides the rest.**

This section is the non-technical tour. Building on veto.fun? Head to the [Developer docs](/developers/overview) for the TypeScript SDK (`@vetodotfun/sdk`), the token-creation integration guide and the full contract reference.

## The promise

Three things make this launchpad worth your time, and none of them are policy. They're properties of the contracts:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Every launch opens equal" icon="scale-balanced" href="/protocol/direct-listing">
    Same opening price for every token, and creators buy at it like everyone else. No presales, no team bags. The contract has no way to do it.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Liquidity locked forever" icon="lock" href="/protocol/venues">
    The entire supply is minted into a vault with no withdrawal function, in the launch transaction itself. Nobody can pull it. Not even us.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Creators keep earning" icon="coins" href="/guides/creator-rewards">
    50% of the pool's swap fees, paid in USDC, from the first trade, for as long as the coin trades.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
