## The promise

Three things make this launchpad worth your time, and none of them are policy. They're properties of the contracts:

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="One curve for everyone" icon="scale-balanced" href="/protocol/bonding-curve">
    Creators buy at the same price as you. No presales, no team bags. The contract has no way to do it.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Liquidity locked forever" icon="lock" href="/protocol/graduation">
    Graduated liquidity is minted into a vault with no withdrawal function. Nobody can pull it. Not even us.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Creators keep earning" icon="coins" href="/guides/creator-rewards">
    0.3% of every curve trade and 30% of Uniswap fees after graduation, for as long as the coin trades.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>
