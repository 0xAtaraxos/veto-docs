If you launched a coin, two streams of SOL accrue to you on-chain. Both are claimed from the same place in your profile, with the same action.

## What you can claim

| Stream | Source | When it accrues |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Trading fees** | 0.2% creator slice of every curve trade | While the coin is on its bonding curve |
| **Creator rewards** | 0.05% creator fee on every Raydium CPMM swap | After graduation, on every CPMM swap |

Accrual is automatic. The claim button just moves what is yours into your spendable wallet balance.

## How to claim

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Go to [liport.fun](https://liport.fun), open your profile, and scroll to the **Creator Rewards** section. Make sure you are connected with the wallet that launched the coin.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find the coin">
    Each row shows the current claimable amount. Coins with zero claimable sink to the bottom.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Preview and claim">
    Hit **Claim** on a row. The preview shows the claimable amount, estimated gas, and the net SOL you will receive. Approve in your wallet. The claim lands in a few seconds.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Or batch claim">
    Hit the batch claim button at the top to claim across many coins at once. Liport bundles them into as few transactions as possible. Big portfolios may need 2 or 3 signatures.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Cadence

Unclaimed balances do not expire. The only reason to claim sooner is to free up capital you want to redeploy.

## Failed claims

A claim can fail for these reasons:

- Not enough SOL to pay gas. Top up a small amount and retry.
- Network congestion. Wait a moment and retry.
- The claim account has not received its first accrual yet (it will not show as claimable in that case).
