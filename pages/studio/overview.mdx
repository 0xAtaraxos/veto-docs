If you launch coins on Liport, you earn on every trade. Curve and post-graduation. You claim from your profile page, in the **Creator Rewards** section.

## Who sees this section

Anyone whose connected wallet has deployed at least one coin on Liport. If you have not launched anything yet, the section is hidden. Launch a coin and it appears.

## Per-coin view

For each coin you launched, the section shows:

| Field | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Coin | Logo, name, symbol |
| Status | On curve / Graduated |
| Claimable | Trading fees plus creator rewards ready to claim |
| Lifetime | Total earned from this coin |
| Actions | Claim, view token page, share PnL |

## Batch claim

If you have many coins with small balances, batch claim rolls multiple claims into as few transactions as possible. Your wallet may show one or more approval prompts depending on the wallet.

## Visibility

Creator reward data is private to your connected wallet. Other people cannot see your unclaimed balances or lifetime totals. Once you claim, the SOL movement is on-chain. Liport does not publish a top earners leaderboard.

If you want to share a single launch's performance, use **Share PnL** on a coin row to generate a card with just that coin's numbers.
