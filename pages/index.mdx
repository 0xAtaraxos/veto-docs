Liport is a Solana launchpad. You connect a wallet, optionally launch a token on a bonding curve, and trade what other people launch. Once a curve fills up, the token moves to a Raydium pool and trades like any other Solana token.

No approvals. No manual listings. You need a wallet, a ticker, and some SOL.

## Why Liport

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Same curve for everyone" icon="chart-line">

  </Card>

## Who it's for

- **Creators** launching a community, meme, or utility token without a developer.
- **Traders** who want early access before a token graduates.
- **Builders** pulling on-chain data via standard Solana RPC, Helius, or Raydium.
