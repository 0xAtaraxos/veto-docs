At Liport, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you access or use our platform and services ("Services").

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Information

When you create a profile and use the platform we collect:

- **Username**. A unique identifier chosen by you.
- **Display Name**. A name displayed publicly on your profile.
- **Bio**. A short description provided by you.
- **Wallet Information**. The public wallet address you connect. We do **not** store your private keys.

### B. Usage Data

We collect certain technical and usage information automatically:

- **Log Data**. Device, browser type, IP address, pages visited, and other interaction data.
- **Cookies**. Used to enhance user experience and gather aggregate usage statistics. We do not track your activity outside the platform.

## 2. Third-Party Services and Analytics

We use Google Analytics and Google reCAPTCHA to improve platform functionality and security.

- **Google Analytics** collects information about interactions with the Platform (IP address, browser type, behavior) and is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Google reCAPTCHA** protects the platform from spam and abuse and is governed by the same policy.

By using the platform, you consent to these services and the collection of data as described.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

- **Provide Services.** Wallet address, username, display name, and bio are used to facilitate interactions and transactions. Username, display name, and bio are publicly visible on your profile.
- **Improve Platform.** Usage data informs performance optimization and feature work.
- **Ensure Security.** Data may be used to detect and prevent fraud or abuse.

## 4. How We Protect Your Information

Liport is non-custodial. We do not store private keys or email addresses. We use industry-standard security measures including encryption of communications between you and the platform, and strict access controls on technical systems.

## 5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal data. We may share information with:

- **Service providers** that operate infrastructure on our behalf (analytics, transaction processing).
- **Legal compliance** recipients when required by law, regulation, or legal process.
- **Business transactions** (merger, acquisition, asset sale) as part of the transaction.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under data protection laws including the EU GDPR, UK GDPR, and the California CCPA/CPRA.

### A. General rights (all users)

- **Access** your personal information.
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete information.
- **Delete** your profile and associated information (subject to our legal obligations).
- **Restrict or object** to certain processing where legally applicable.
- **Portability**. Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

### B. GDPR / UK GDPR

- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
- Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

### C. California (CCPA / CPRA)

- Know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
- Access specific pieces held about you.
- Delete personal information (subject to legal exceptions).
- We do **not** sell or share personal data as defined by CCPA/CPRA.
- You will not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising these rights.

### Exercising Your Rights

Email [support@liport.fun](mailto:support@liport.fun). We may ask for limited information to verify your identity before acting on a request.

## 7. Third-Party Links

The platform may link to third-party sites we do not operate. Their privacy practices are their own. Review their policies before sharing information.

## 8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies for analytics, reCAPTCHA, and to enhance navigation. You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Doing so may affect functionality.

## 9. Data Retention for Analytics and reCAPTCHA

Data collected through Google Analytics and Google reCAPTCHA is retained as long as necessary for statistical analysis and fraud prevention purposes.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The platform is not intended for individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors and will delete it promptly if discovered.

## 11. International Transfers

Your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries outside your country of residence. By using the platform you consent to those transfers.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes are posted here with a new "Last Updated" date. Review periodically.
