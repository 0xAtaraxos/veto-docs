Before you start, you need a Solana wallet (Phantom, Backpack, Solflare or any Wallet Standard wallet) with a small amount of SOL for network fees.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Open [liport.fun](https://liport.fun) and hit **Connect Wallet** in the top right. Pick your wallet, approve, and sign the short message Liport asks for. The signature is free, off-chain, and only proves the wallet is yours. It does not move any funds.

    More detail in [Connect Wallet](/platform/connect-wallet).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Find a token">
    Use **Discover** or the top nav coins list. The filters all look at the same set of coins from different angles:

    - **New** for fresh launches
    - **Trending** for activity over a rolling window
    - **Movers** for biggest price swings
    - **Last Traded**, **Market Cap**, **Featured**, **Explore**, **Recommended** for the rest

    Click a card to open the token page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Make your first trade">
    On a token page you get a chart, a buy/sell box, and a live trade feed.

    1. Pick **Buy** or **Sell**.
    2. Enter an amount in SOL or in the token.
    3. Check the expected output (slippage and fees included).
    4. Hit **Swap** and approve in your wallet.

    The widget routes through the bonding curve or the Raydium pool automatically, depending on whether the token has graduated yet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="(Optional) Launch a token">
    Hit **Create** in the top nav and fill in:

    - Name and symbol
    - A square logo (PNG or JPG)
    - Description and any social links
    - An optional first buy in SOL

    Approve the single transaction. Your token shows up on Discover in seconds and the link is ready to share.

    Full guide: [Launching a Token](/platform/launching-a-token).
  </Step>

  <Step title="(Optional) Claim earnings">
    If you launched a coin and it has trade volume, open your **Profile**. The Creator Rewards section lists what you can claim per coin. Claim one at a time or all at once.

    Full guide: [Earning as a Creator](/studio/overview).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Keep a few tenths of a SOL on hand for transaction fees. Almost every action on Liport costs a tiny bit of network gas.
</Tip>
