The token page is where most trading and research happens. Every section reflects the on-chain state of the token.

## Header

Top strip with the logo, name, symbol and contract address (click to copy). Next to that: market cap, 24h stats, a favourite star and a Solscan link.

## Chart

Candles are OHLCV (open, high, low, close, volume), pulled from Raydium's pool history through the Liport backend. Intervals: `1m`, `5m`, `15m`, `1h`, `4h`, `1d`, depending on what Raydium has.

You can toggle the view between **Price** and **Market Cap**, and between **SOL** and **USD** as the quote currency.

## Buy / sell widget

See [Trading](/platform/trading) for the full mechanics. The widget shows:

- Your SOL balance and token balance
- Quote amount and slippage estimate
- Fee breakdown (curve vs CPMM)
- Graduation progress (when on the curve)

## Live trades

Every buy and sell lands in the trades table below the chart. Columns: time, wallet (truncated, click to open their profile), side, SOL amount, token amount, price, tx link.

## Holders

Top holders by balance, pulled through Helius. Each row shows:

- Wallet (click to open the profile)
- Balance and approximate % of supply

<Tip>
The top-holder list is a fast way to spot unusual concentration. The curve and pool accounts often sit at the top. They are program-owned, not whales.
</Tip>

## Metadata

Below the main sections, the token page shows:

- The creator's description
- Social links (website, X, Telegram) if provided at launch
- Creation timestamp and the deployer wallet
- Explorer links (Solscan)

## When the token has graduated

A "Graduated" badge replaces the graduation bar, and trades route through the CPMM. Everything else on the page works the same.
