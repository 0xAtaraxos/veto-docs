Liport is non-custodial. Your keys stay in your wallet. The site can only do what you sign for.

## Supported wallets

Any wallet that implements the [Solana Wallet Standard](https://docs.solana.com/wallet-guide). The ones in regular use on Liport:

- **Phantom** (browser extension and mobile)
- **Backpack**
- **Solflare**
- **Glow**
- **Ledger** via Phantom or Solflare

Hardware wallets work anywhere a software wallet does, with the usual confirm-on-device step.

## Connecting

1. Hit **Connect Wallet** in the top right.
2. Pick your wallet.
3. Approve the connection in the wallet.
4. Sign the Liport sign-in message.

The sign-in message is not a transaction. It costs no gas, moves no tokens and grants no allowances. It only proves the wallet is yours so the backend can issue you a session.

## What the session does

After you sign, the backend sets a short-lived JWT in an HttpOnly cookie. The session is used for:

- Marking favourites
- Editing your profile
- Claiming creator rewards from the profile page

Public reads (Discover, token pages, trades, charts) work without a session.

Hit **Disconnect** to end the session.

## Switching wallets

Disconnect, then connect again with the new wallet. Favourites and profile are tied to the wallet address, so a different wallet means a different profile.

## What Liport cannot do

- Move funds without your signature.
- Sign transactions for you.
- See or store your seed phrase.
- Set silent token allowances. Solana has no ERC-20 style infinite approvals.

## Troubleshooting

**"Wallet not detected"**. Make sure the extension is installed and enabled. Some wallet extensions sleep after long idle periods. Reload the page.

**Sign-in message loops**. Clear the session cookie and try again. If your wallet switched accounts mid-session, the backend invalidates the old session.

**Ledger signing stalls**. Turn on "Blind Signing" in the Solana app on the device. Long transactions like graduation or batch claim can go past the device's short signing mode.
