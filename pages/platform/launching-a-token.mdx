Launching is meant to be quick. You do not need a Solana dev environment. You do not need to know what an SPL mint is. If you can connect a wallet and approve a transaction, you can launch.

## Before you start

You will need:

- A Solana wallet with enough SOL for gas (plus any optional first buy).
- A name and a symbol. Symbols are usually 2 to 10 characters.
- A square logo (PNG or JPG, ideally 256x256 or larger).
- Optional: description and social links.

## Step by step

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Create flow">
    Hit **Create** in the top nav.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fill in the basics">
    - **Name**. Shown across the site.
    - **Symbol / Ticker**. Shown next to the price.
    - **Logo**. Drag and drop or file picker. The image is pinned to IPFS through Pinata.
    - **Description**. Shown on the token page.
    - **Social links**. X, Telegram, website. All optional.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure your first buy (optional)">
    You can include an initial buy in the same transaction. That gives you the first position at the lowest curve price and shows the room you are putting skin in the game.

    - Enter a SOL amount.
    - The widget shows the expected token amount at current curve parameters.
    - Leave it at zero to launch with no position and buy later.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and submit">
    The confirm panel shows:

    - Total SOL cost (gas plus optional buy)
    - Your token balance if you included a buy
    - Graduation target (85 SOL)
    - Curve fee parameters

    Hit **Launch Token** and approve in your wallet.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share the link">
    Your token shows up on Discover in seconds. The URL looks like:

    ```
    https://liport.fun/coin/<mint-address>
    ```

    Share the mint address or the URL in your communities. Both go to the same page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After launch

- The token shows up in your [Profile](/platform/profile).
- Trading fees start accruing to your claim account on the first buy.
- Creator rewards are claimable from the Creator Rewards section of your profile.

## What you cannot change after launch

Fixed by the on-chain program:

- Total supply
- Decimals
- Curve parameters and graduation target
- Fee rates
- The creator wallet (the deployer)

You can edit the metadata (name, image, description, socials) later. The economics are locked.
