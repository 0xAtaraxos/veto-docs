## Fair launch

- **No presale.** There are no private rounds, whitelists, or early access. Trading opens on Uniswap for everyone at the same moment.
- **On-chain and public.** Every buy and sell is visible on Robinhood Chain from the first block, including any team activity.
- **Standard ERC-20.** No transfer taxes, no pause, no blacklist.

## Buybacks

**50% of all fees the hoodstar.fun launchpad generates are used to buy back \$STAR** on the open market. As the platform grows and more tokens launch and trade, half of every fee the protocol earns flows back into \$STAR — aligning the token directly with the success of the launchpad.
