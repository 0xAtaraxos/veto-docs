Every token on maki.finance lives the same on-chain life. No special cases, no admin shortcuts, no "trust us". And that life has exactly one phase: **launched**. There is no bonding curve to fill, no graduation to wait for, no migration that can fail.

## Launch: one transaction, everything

A creator calls the launchpad with a **name**, **ticker**, and **metadata URI** (image and description, pinned to IPFS), picks a venue, and confirms. One transaction:

- Deploys the token: exactly 1,000,000,000 tokens (18 decimals), minted once. No owner, no mint function, no pause, no blacklist, no transfer fees.
- Opens a **WETH/token pool on SushiSwap or Uniswap v3** (the creator's choice, permanent) at the fixed opening price every launch shares: FDV ≈ 1.4982 ETH.
- Locks **the entire supply** as a single liquidity position in an immutable vault. Nobody can ever withdraw it; it earns the pool's 1% swap fees instead.
- Optionally executes the creator's **dev buy** at the public price, before anyone else can trade.

Cost: a flat **0.001 ETH** plus gas. The creator receives **zero** free tokens.

## Trading: a real DEX pool, from block one

The token trades like any other v3 token because it is one:

- Trade it on maki.finance (exact quotes, native ETH, slippage protection), or through any wallet, aggregator, or bot that supports SushiSwap or Uniswap on Robinhood Chain.
- Charting and discovery platforms index the pool from its first swap. Your token is born visible to the whole ecosystem, not parked in a walled garden.
- **The opening price is a permanent floor**: no liquidity exists above it, so sells alone can never push the price above the launch price, and the pool always holds the ETH buyers put in.

## Earning: the 1% works for creator and protocol

Every swap pays the pool's 1% fee into the locked position. Anyone entitled can trigger a harvest at any time, and it splits at the ratio snapshotted into the token at launch: **30% to the creator, 70% to the protocol treasury**, forever. That's the whole business model, and it only pays when tokens actually trade. See [Fees](/protocol/fees).

## What can never happen

| Action | Possible? |
| --- | --- |
| Creator or team mints extra supply | ❌ No mint function exists |
| Trading gets paused or wallets blacklisted | ❌ No pause or blacklist exists |
| Liquidity gets pulled | ❌ Locked in an ownerless vault with no withdrawal function |
| Sells dump the price below the launch price | ❌ No liquidity exists above the opening tick |
| A token's fee terms change after launch | ❌ Snapshotted at creation, immutable |
| Platform touches your funds | ❌ Fully non-custodial |
