Let's be honest about the category. Since pump.fun proved a launchpad could bootstrap an idea from nothing, launchpads have multiplied faster than the tokens on them. The mechanism is a commodity now. Anyone can spin one up in a weekend, and most of them differ in logo only.

So why build *another* one?

## Because "fair" became a marketing word

We kept watching the same movie. A launchpad promises fair launches, and the promise is enforced by nothing:

- Team wallets that can pause trading when it suits them.
- Liquidity "locked" for 90 days, which is just a rug with a calendar.
- Fee terms that quietly change after your token is already live.
- Insider allocations dressed up as "ecosystem funds".
- A walled-garden phase where the token only exists on the launchpad's own site, invisible to the rest of the market.

None of that is a bug in those platforms. It's what happens when fairness is a policy instead of a property.

## Fairness as structure

Maki's answer is to remove the trust from the equation entirely. Not "we won't", but **"we can't"**:

- **Every launch opens identically.** Same opening price, same locked-supply pool, for every token including anything we launch. There is no function for presales or team allocations, so there are no presales or team allocations.
- **Liquidity locked forever, from block one.** The entire supply is minted as liquidity straight into a vault with no withdrawal function. Not timelocked. Gone, permanently, for everyone including us.
- **Fee terms frozen at launch.** Every token snapshots its fee split the moment it's created. Nobody, including the protocol owner, can change an existing token's terms. Ever.
- **No walled garden.** Tokens list directly on SushiSwap or Uniswap, so every wallet, aggregator, and charting platform on the chain can see and trade them from the first swap. We compete on the app, not on locking your token into it.

The full list of what can never happen is in the [security model](/protocol/security). It's a short read because the admin surface is nearly empty, and that's the point.

## Because every chain gets one shot at this

Robinhood Chain is new: an Arbitrum Orbit L2 with ETH gas, fast blocks, and no incumbent. Every chain gets one defining launchpad, and it's decided early. We built Maki natively for this chain, from the contracts up, working closely with the DEX infrastructure that's already here, to be that.
