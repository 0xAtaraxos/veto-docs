veto.fun runs on **Arc**, Circle's EVM-compatible Layer 1. Any standard Ethereum wallet works: MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, Rainbow, or any injected/WalletConnect wallet. No email signup is required, though email and social login are available through an embedded wallet if you prefer.

## Add Arc to your wallet

Your wallet needs Arc added as a network. The app prompts you to add it automatically when you connect; to add it manually, use:

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Network name | Arc |
| RPC URL | `https://rpc.mainnet.arc.io` |
| Chain ID | `5042` |
| Currency symbol | USDC |
| Block explorer | `https://explorer.arc.io` |

<Note>
  Gas on Arc is paid in **USDC**, and so is every trade. There is no separate gas token to hold: the same USDC balance pays for the transaction (about $0.01) and for the coin you're buying. Bridge USDC to Arc from any chain Circle's CCTP supports, or withdraw USDC to the Arc network from an exchange that lists it.
</Note>

## Connect

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open veto.fun">
    Click **Login** in the top-right corner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your wallet">
    Choose your wallet and approve the connection request. If prompted, approve adding/switching to Arc.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Sign the login message. It's a plain signature (no transaction, no gas) that proves you control the wallet. veto.fun never asks for your seed phrase and never requests token approvals at login.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You're in. Your USDC balance shows in the navbar; click it for tips on getting USDC onto Arc.

## Security

- We never ask for private keys or seed phrases. Anyone who does is not us.
- Login is a signature over a human-readable message, not a transaction.
- Trading a token requires a one-time token approval for our swap router. Check the address against the [contract list](/developers/overview#contract-addresses) if in doubt.
- Bookmark [veto.fun](https://veto.fun) and check the URL before signing anything.
