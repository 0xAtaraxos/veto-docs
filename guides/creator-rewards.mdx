Launching a token on veto.fun gives you a permanent, on-chain fee stream. Not an airdrop, not an allocation you have to dump: a revenue share that pays as long as your coin trades. And because your token trades in a real Uniswap pool from block one, the stream starts with its very first swap.

## The stream

Your token's entire supply sits as locked liquidity in its [Uniswap v3 pool](/protocol/venues). Every swap pays the pool's **1% fee**, that fee accrues to the locked position, and each harvest is split:

| Recipient | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Creator (you, or the wallet you choose) | 50% |
| Protocol treasury | 50% |

Your token's fee terms are **snapshotted on-chain the moment you launch** and can never change afterwards. Not by you, not by the platform. (The platform can tune the rates offered to *future* launches.)

Every swap of your token, from any interface, aggregator, or bot on Arc, earns you fees. This stream has no expiry, and payouts arrive in **USDC** and, when the position has collected token-side fees, in your token as well.

## Choosing who gets paid

On the create page, **Fees go to** lets you keep the stream yourself or route it to any other wallet: a team multisig, a friend, a community treasury. The recipient sees the token in their own studio and can claim without you.

The current recipient can redirect the stream again later from the studio. It is always the **whole** creator share that moves; there is no partial split between two wallets.

<Warning>
  Redirecting the fee stream is irreversible unless the *new* recipient redirects it back. Double-check the address.
</Warning>

## Claiming

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your studio">
    Go to **Studio** on [veto.fun](https://veto.fun). Wallets that launched a token, or that a fee stream was routed to, see every such token with claimable and lifetime earnings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim">
    Hit claim on a token (or claim across your portfolio). One signature; the fee locker harvests the pool fees and pays your share out directly, in USDC.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Claims are **pull-based and protected**: only the registered fee recipient, the treasury, or the platform's claimer service can trigger a payout, and the money only ever goes to the recipient; no caller can redirect it. The platform periodically batch-claims on creators' behalf, so fees may simply arrive in your wallet without you doing anything.
</Note>

## For developers

Integrating creation into your own product, and want the fee stream to land in your wallet? See [Integrate token creation](/developers/launch-integration). Who a token pays is public: `GET /api/launchpad/recipient/:token`.

## What creators can't do

For traders' protection, creators have **no special powers** over their token: no free supply, no minting, no pausing, no fee changes, no liquidity withdrawal. Your only privileges are the fee stream and the optional [dev buy](/protocol/direct-listing#the-dev-buy) at the public opening price.
