Launching a token on maki.finance gives you a permanent, on-chain fee stream. Not an airdrop, not an allocation you have to dump: a revenue share that pays as long as your coin trades. And because your token trades in a real DEX pool from block one, the stream starts with its very first swap.

## The stream

Your token's entire supply sits as locked liquidity in its [SushiSwap or Uniswap pool](/protocol/venues). Every swap pays the pool's **1% fee**, that fee accrues to the locked position, and each harvest is split:

| Recipient | Share |
| --- | --- |
| Creator (you) | 30% |
| Protocol treasury | 70% |

Your token's fee terms are **snapshotted on-chain the moment you launch** and can never change afterwards. Not by you, not by the platform. (The platform can tune the rates offered to *future* launches.)

Every swap of your token, from any interface, aggregator, or bot on Robinhood Chain, earns you fees. This stream has no expiry, and payouts arrive in ETH and, when the position has collected token-side fees, in your token as well.

## Claiming

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your dashboard">
    Go to your profile on [maki.finance](https://maki.finance). Wallets that have launched at least one token see a creator dashboard with claimable and lifetime earnings per token.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim">
    Hit claim on a token (or claim across your portfolio). One signature; the FeeLocker harvests the pool fees and pays your share out directly.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Claims are **pull-based and protected**: only you (the registered fee recipient) or the platform's claimer service can trigger a payout, and the money only ever goes to you; no caller can redirect it. The platform periodically batch-claims on creators' behalf, so fees may simply arrive in your wallet without you doing anything.
</Note>

## Redirecting your fee stream

The current fee recipient of a token can reassign the stream to a different address. This is useful for routing earnings to a multisig, a splitter contract, or a new wallet.

<Warning>
  Redirecting the fee stream is irreversible unless the *new* recipient redirects it back. Double-check the address.
</Warning>

## What creators can't do

For traders' protection, creators have **no special powers** over their token: no free supply, no minting, no pausing, no fee changes, no liquidity withdrawal. Your only privileges are the fee stream and the optional [dev buy](/protocol/direct-listing#the-dev-buy) at the public opening price.
